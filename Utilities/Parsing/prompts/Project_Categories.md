# Project Categories

## Retrospective
No direct interactions with individual participants.

### Negligible Risk

#### Retrospective Data
- De-identified data from repository
- Identifiable data from repository

#### Retrospective Biospecimen
- Anonymous cell lines
- De-identified biospecimens from repository
- Identifiable biospecimens from repository

---

## Prospective Observational
Minimal direct interactions with non-patient or patient participants.

### Minimal Risk

#### Surveys / Education
- Anonymous surveys
- Identifiable surveys (non-sensitive data)
- Research involving non-patient healthcare providers and educators

### More than Minimal Risk

#### Sensitive Data / Clinical Interactions
- Identifiable surveys (sensitive data)
- Vulnerable populations
- Participant interactions limited to blood testing, imaging, etc.

---

## Prospective Interventional
Health-related interventions involving non-patient or patient participants.

### More than Minimal Risk

#### Lower Risk
- Benign behavioural interventions
- Trials and other studies testing standard-of-care (SOC) products or techniques

#### Higher Risk
- Clinical trials involving experimental products

---
