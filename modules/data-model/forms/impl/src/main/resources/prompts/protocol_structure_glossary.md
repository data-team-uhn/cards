Rubric glossary for routing:
B.1: General information — title, sponsor, PI/signatories, synopsis.
B.2: Background — rationale, prior evidence, risks/benefits background, population background.
B.3: Objectives — study aims, hypotheses, scientific purpose.
B.4: Trial design — endpoints, design, randomization, blinding, schedule, duration, stopping rules.
B.5: Participants — sample size, population source, inclusion/exclusion, screening, recruitment.
B.6: Withdrawal — discontinuation, withdrawal, replacement, follow-up after withdrawal.
B.7: Treatment/intervention — study treatments, dosing, concomitant meds, adherence, randomization procedure.
B.8: Efficacy — efficacy parameters and assessment methods.
B.9: Safety — safety parameters, adverse events, SAE reporting, safety follow-up, DSMP.
B.10: Statistics — sample size calculation, analysis methods, missing data, analysis populations.
B.11: Source records — monitoring, audits, direct access, source documents.
B.12: Quality — monitoring, QA/QC, protocol compliance, staff training.
B.13: Ethics — REB, informed consent, ethical standards, study discontinuation.
B.14: Data handling — data collection, confidentiality, storage, retention, source data.
B.15: Financing — funding, budget, insurance.
B.16: Publication — publication and authorship policy.
B.17: References/appendix — bibliography, cited literature, supplementary materials.
